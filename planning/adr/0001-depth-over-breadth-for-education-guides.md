# Depth over breadth for education Guides

The education databases hold 76 Entries, but only 29 are published as Entry Guides. That gap is deliberate, not unfinished work.

The education surface exists to win long-tail informational search. Measured against the fields their templates actually render, published Guides carried a median of 84 words (clouds), 86 (weather systems) and 186 (phenomena) — the entire corpus was 8,317 words across all three databases. Publishing the remaining 47 Entries at that depth would have added 47 more thin pages to one subfolder, which is the pattern Google's Helpful Content system demotes at the *section* level. The downside case was losing the 29 URLs that already work in order to add 47 that would not.

So: hold the Guide count, take each Guide to roughly 900 words, and give the remaining Entries coverage through a small number of Collection Guides grouped by theme rather than a page each. Entries without a Guide are a legitimate state — they are index rows on an Atlas.

## Consequences

Anyone who notices that 47 Entries have no page and "fixes" it by generating a page per Entry will reintroduce exactly the problem this decision avoids. Coverage gaps get closed with Collection Guides, or with an Entry Guide written to full depth — never by lowering the depth bar.
