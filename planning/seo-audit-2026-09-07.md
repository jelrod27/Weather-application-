# SEO audit — 16bitweather.co — 2026-09-07

Sources: Google Search Console (domain property `sc-domain:16bitweather.co`, data 2026-03-29 → 2026-09-05), a read-only static audit of this repo at `f9386bb`, a full live crawl of the 138-URL sitemap plus host/redirect/404 probes, and local Lighthouse lab runs (the keyless PageSpeed Insights API was over quota). Nothing in the repo or in Search Console was changed. Raw notes live in the session scratchpad; this file is the durable record.

## 1. Verdict

The site is technically indexable, fast to respond, and has no penalties, but it earns almost nothing from search: **18 clicks on 4,590 impressions in 16 months (0.4% CTR, average position 35.9)**. Impressions have roughly doubled month over month since July while clicks stayed at about one a week, which means Google is testing the site on pages 3–6 for real queries and readers never see it.

Three things explain most of that, in order of leverage:

1. **Authority is zero.** Search Console knows of 3 external links in total (2 from github.com, 1 from x.com), all to the homepage. Every query the site surfaces for — "space weather live", "solar flare monitor", "boston climate", "anticyclones" — is owned by NOAA, spaceweatherlive.com, Wikipedia, and the big weather brands. No on-page work moves a position-50 page to page 1 against those without links.
2. **Google is actively declining to index the pages that matter.** 21 of the 45 city pages are "Crawled – currently not indexed" or "Discovered – currently not indexed", including Chicago, Los Angeles, San Francisco, Houston, Phoenix, Philadelphia, Seattle, San Diego, Austin, Portland, Washington DC. Several of those (SF, LA, Chicago, Jacksonville, Orlando) *had* impressions earlier this year and have since been dropped, which is Google's quality filter deciding the templated city pages are not worth keeping. The `/cloud-types` and `/weather-systems` hubs are in the same bucket.
3. **Googlebot cannot render the site.** `public/robots.txt` disallows `/_next/`, which blocks every JS chunk, CSS file and `/_next/image` URL. Search Console confirms it (19 `/_next/static` URLs under "Blocked by robots.txt"). Any content that only exists after hydration — the home hub, news, winter, aviation, stargazer, the live weather card on every city page — is invisible to indexing, and CSS-less rendering hurts mobile-usability signals. This is a one-line fix and it very plausibly feeds finding 2.

Below that sit a set of medium technical defects (broken blog structured data, three `<h1>`s per page, titles far over 60 characters, a content-free homepage, 150 orphaned deep-sky pages making up 57% of the index) and a content strategy that spreads thin over many intents instead of owning a few.

## 3. Technical findings (code and live)

Severity is SEO impact; effort is S (hours), M (a day or two), L (a week+).

### Critical

**T1 — `robots.txt` blocks `/_next/`.** `public/robots.txt:28`. Blocks all JS, CSS and `/_next/image`. Google's guidance is explicit that blocked JS/CSS prevents rendering. Delete the line; keep `/api/`. `__tests__/robots-seo.test.ts` does not assert it, so the change is test-safe. Effort S.

### High

**T2 — Blog Article JSON-LD is emitted as a `<meta>` tag.** `app/blog/[slug]/page.tsx:56-58`, `app/radar/layout.tsx:55-57`, `app/news/layout.tsx:50-52` put JSON-LD in `metadata.other['application/ld+json']`; Next renders `other` as `<meta name="application/ld+json" content="…">`. Verified live on `/blog/volcanoes-2026-09-02` and `/radar`. All 44 posts have no parsable Article schema; `__tests__/blog-jsonld.test.ts:38-39` asserts the broken shape. Move to a `<script type="application/ld+json">` via `safeJsonLd` in the page body, add `image` (the `/api/og/blog` URL) and a real `dateModified`. Effort S.

**T3 — Three `<h1>` elements on every page.** `components/navigation.tsx:93` and `:184` wrap the brand in `<h1>` for the desktop and mobile variants; `PageWrapper` renders the nav on nearly every route. City pages get a fourth from `components/hero-weather-card.tsx:86`. Change the nav brand to a `<div>`/`<a>`; keep one H1 per page. Effort S.

**T4 — Title and description lengths.** 16 of 21 hub titles exceed 60 characters (glossary 88, warnings 81, tropical 77, space-weather 76, earth-sciences 76); 31 of 44 blog titles exceed 60 once ` | 16 Bit Weather Blog` is appended; all 10 priority-city titles run 61–69. 12 descriptions exceed 160 (earth-sciences 211, glossary 193). Add `title: { default, template: '%s | 16 Bit Weather' }` to `app/layout.tsx`, drop the hand-appended suffixes, and rewrite the long ones. Also fixes the "16 Bit" vs "16-Bit" brand drift. Effort M.

**T5 — The homepage server HTML has no copy.** `app/page.tsx:125-186`, `app/home-client.tsx`: a visually hidden H1, a search box skeleton, "PRESS START…", the footer and 12 city links. Nothing supports the title "Live Weather, Radar & Space Weather". `/radar` and `/space-weather` already have SEO-content components; give the homepage one (what the site is, the four main tools, links into the clusters). Effort M.

**T6 — 150 deep-sky pages are orphaned yet indexed.** `app/sitemap.ts:89-91` omits them on purpose; the only links come from `components/stargazer/DeepSkyHighlights.tsx` after a client fetch. Either add a server-rendered catalog index page plus the URLs to the sitemap (they already rank on page 1 for object names), or `noindex` them. Recommendation: keep and link — they are the one place the site already wins low-competition queries. Effort S–M.

### Medium

**T7 — Duplicate blog titles.** 7 posts are titled exactly "This Week in Weather" (2026-04-27 → 06-21); 9 summaries are under 70 characters (one is 21). Effort S.

**T8 — Sitemap `lastmod` is dishonest for shells.** Hourly for `/radar`, `/severe`, `/warnings`, `/stargazer`, `/aviation`, `/earth-sciences` whose HTML never changes; weekly for 45 static city pages. Google stops trusting lastmod site-wide once it sees this. Keep hourly only on `/space-weather`, use monthly or deploy date elsewhere. Effort S.

**T9 — Structured-data quality.** City pages declare `@type: 'WeatherForecast'` (not a schema.org type, `app/weather/[city]/page.tsx:88`); `/cloud-types` FAQ says "13 cloud types" while the page renders 35 and the FAQ Q&As are not visible on the page (`app/cloud-types/layout.tsx:10,47-50`); `/weather-systems` uses `Article` on a listing page; Organization `logo` is an SVG favicon; no `sameAs`. Effort S.

**T10 — Two competing homepage OG images.** File-based `app/opengraph-image.tsx` wins over the config `HOMEPAGE_OG_IMAGE`; the file says "Retro Terminal Weather Forecast", the config says "Live Forecasts, Radar & Space Weather". Delete one. Effort S.

**T11 — Blog `?tag=` links to a filter that does not exist.** `app/blog/[slug]/blog-article.tsx:92` links tags; `app/blog/page.tsx` reads only `category`. 37 such URLs are in Google's canonical-duplicate bucket. Either implement tag pages (server-rendered, indexable, unique intro copy) or stop linking tags. Effort S–M.

**T12 — Blog pagination is client state.** `app/blog/blog-index.tsx:25,33`: no `?page=` URLs, so posts 11–44 are not linked from `/blog` HTML. Effort S.

**T13 — robots `Disallow` and `noindex` on the same private routes.** `/dashboard`, `/profile`, `/auth/`, `/radar-diagnostic`, `/gfs-model/` are both blocked and noindexed; Google cannot see the noindex on a blocked URL. Drop the Disallows (keep `/api/`). `/auth/login?next=/dashboard` already has an impression. Effort S.

**T14 — `/warnings/[id]` pages are indexable while live and 404 after expiry.** Add `noindex` or `unavailable_after`. Effort S.

**T15 — RSS is undiscoverable.** `/blog/rss.xml` is valid but nothing links it: no `alternates.types['application/rss+xml']` on the blog layout. Effort S.

**T16 — Dead preconnects and unused font family.** `app/layout.tsx:150-160` preconnects fonts.googleapis/gstatic (never used with `next/font`) and basemap tile hosts on every page; a fourth family (JetBrains Mono) loads via `styles/fonts.css`. Move tile preconnects into the map layouts; audit weights. Effort S.

### Found by the live crawl

**T17 — `Disallow: /api/` blocks every social and article image (High).** All 136 `og:image` values on the site are `/api/og?…` or `/api/og/blog?…`, and the 29 education Article blocks use the same URL as `image`. Googlebot-Image obeys the `*` group, so none of them can be fetched for Discover, image search or Article rich results. Add `Allow: /api/og` above `Disallow: /api/` in the `*` group and in the four AI-search groups. Effort S.

**T18 — No server-rendered H1 on any city page, `/warnings` or `/aviation` (High).** The hero H1 on city pages arrives with the client fetch; the server HTML's page-specific heading is the `<h2>` "X Climate Guide". Combined with T3, Google sees two brand H1s and no topic H1 on all 45 city pages. Render the city H1 server-side in `app/weather/[city]/page.tsx`. Effort S.

**T19 — Thirteen tool and hub pages ship under 300 server-rendered words (Medium).** `/warnings` and `/aviation` send 29 words with `<main>` containing only "Loading…"; `/winter` 38, `/news` 41, `/severe` 45, `/stargazer` 56, `/earth-sciences` 67, home 82, `/alerts` 172 (its `<main>` is "Loading Bitwatch…", the form copy sits after the footer). `/radar` is the working pattern: three paragraphs plus a four-question FAQ above the client map. Live data cannot be pre-rendered, but the evergreen explainer can. Effort M.

**T20 — Global nav links a noindexed page and a redirect on all 137 pages (Medium).** "Tools → Hourly" links `/hourly` (noindex, canonical to home, `no-store`), and the footer "Map" links `/map`, a 307 to `/radar`. Meanwhile `/alerts` (sitemap priority 0.9) has zero inlinks from any page, and 19 blog posts have zero inlinks because `/blog` renders only the 10 newest cards with no pagination. Effort S.

**T21 — Soft 404s (Medium).** Any `/weather/<anything>` returns 200 with a noindex shell (`/weather/notacity-xyz`, `/weather/New-York-NY`, `/weather/new-york`), and `/index` serves the home page as a 200 with canonical to `/`. Return `notFound()` for slugs outside the catalog (or 308 known aliases to the canonical slug) and 308 `/index` to `/`. Effort S.

**T22 — `favicon.ico` is a single byte (Medium).** `/favicon.ico` is 1 byte (0x20), served `immutable` for a year, and declared first in `<head>`. Google's favicon crawler may pick it. Replace with a real 32×32 ICO or drop the link and keep the SVG. Effort S.

**T23 — Apex redirect is a 307 and a two-hop chain (Low).** `http://16bitweather.co/` → 308 → `https://16bitweather.co/` → **307** → `https://www.16bitweather.co/`. The apex-to-www hop is Vercel's default domain redirect (temporary); set it to 308 in the Vercel domain settings. The apex HSTS header also lacks `includeSubDomains; preload`. Effort S.

**T24 — Layout shift on the pages with client widgets (Medium).** Lighthouse mobile CLS: `/warnings` 1.144, `/weather/new-york-ny` 0.688 (0.703 desktop); every other measured page is 0–0.125. The live weather card and warning list push the climate guide and footer down when they load. Reserve space for the widgets. Effort M.

**T25 — Twelve blog titles are mid-word truncations of the first sentence (Medium).** Examples: "Two volcanoes on U.S", "…at Vostok Station in". These come from the newsletter pipeline's title step; the longest `<title>` on the site is 132 characters. Fix the generator in `scripts/newsletter/` and rename the affected posts. Effort S.

**T26 — `/blog` ships 388 KB of HTML, 347 KB of it inline RSC payload, for 10 cards (Low).** The page is request-time dynamic because it reads `searchParams`. Effort S–M.

### What is already right

Absolute canonicals and OG on every indexable route; thorough `noindex` coverage pinned by tests; the sitemap is generated from the same data as the routes with parity tests and no redirect-only URLs; the Education Guide SEO builder (`lib/education/guide-seo.ts`) is the best-implemented pattern on the site; `/radar` and `/space-weather` ship server-rendered explanatory copy; city pages use ISR with FAQ and monthly tables; apex → www 308; all images have `alt`; analytics and Sentry are lazy; `llms.txt` and an explicit AI-crawler policy exist; the 404 page returns a real 404.

## 5. Content findings

**City pages.** Two cities (Boston, Atlanta) have hand-written climate copy and they are the two city pages Google ranks and clicks. The other 43 share a template that differs only by name, and Google's response is to index 27, hold 21 out, and drop ones it previously indexed. The 46-city enrichment table (`faqs`, `monthlyHighs`, `seasons`) only covers some of them. The Boston page is proof the format works: a "snow patterns" query at position 3.8 and "weather in boston all year round" at 6.0.

**Space weather.** One page carries 848 impressions across a dozen distinct intents (flare monitor, Kp index, solar wind, aurora, forecast, "live"). The competitors have a page per intent. The 14 space-weather API routes already exist, so per-intent landing pages are mostly copy and layout.

**Education.** Guides are well-built but nearly unlinked: `anticyclones` gets 149 impressions at position 55.8 with zero internal links except the hub. The glossary gets 307 impressions because it covers many terms. Guides also lack `BreadcrumbList` on the atlas pages and have 11 of 16 summaries over 160 characters.

**Blog.** 44 posts, none thin (median 901 words), 38 indexed, ranking 5–16 for their own long-tail phrasing but drawing 2–50 impressions each. Weekly recaps are not a search asset; the evergreen explainers (ocean currents, historical events, paleoclimate) are the ones that get impressions.

**Alerts.** "nws alerts by email", "weather alerts by email", "storm alerts by email" are commercial-intent queries with low competition where `/alerts` already appears at 40–60. The page is a form with almost no copy.
