# 16-Bit Weather

Vocabulary for understanding weather at a visitor's selected place. Established alerting and education terms remain defined in [CONTEXT.md](./CONTEXT.md).

## Language

**Selected Location**:
The place whose weather a visitor is currently viewing. It can differ from the visitor's physical location or a place they monitor for weather warnings.
_Avoid_: Your location, Protected Place

**Sky Estimate**:
A weather-model estimate of cloud cover and related sky conditions near the Selected Location at a stated time. It describes estimated conditions rather than a direct view or confirmed identification of the clouds overhead.
_Avoid_: Live sky observation, detected clouds

**Sky Illustration**:
A small scientific illustration that matches the conditions and time described in a sky explanation. It is an explanatory drawing, not a measured image of the Selected Location.
_Avoid_: Live sky view, cloud detection

**Sky Window**:
The current moment through approximately the next two hours for the Selected Location. It includes the current Sky Estimate and distinctly labeled expectations for the near future.
_Avoid_: Two-hour data-age limit, recent weather history
