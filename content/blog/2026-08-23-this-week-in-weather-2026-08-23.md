The most concentrated tornado sequence unfolded on the evening of August 22 into early August 23 (EDT/local). A warning for Cuyahoga and Lorain counties in Ohio dropped at 5:01 PM EDT on August 22. Three hours later, separate warnings fired for Centre and Clearfield counties in Pennsylvania — the first at 7:58 PM EDT, a second at 8:11 PM EDT, two distinct cells or circulations requiring back-to-back coverage from the State College (CTP) office within 13 minutes. That timing and that precipitation volume suggest a front or shortwave delivering multiple rounds of convection rather than a single organized system. The region's flash flood exposure is already elevated after the past week's 239 flash flood warnings nationally, and another multi-inch rainfall event in urban and suburban terrain will put storm drain infrastructure under pressure again. Watch the August 27 window closely; if a slow-moving front stalls over the mid-Atlantic, the rainfall totals could concentrate in a way the weekly average doesn't capture.
